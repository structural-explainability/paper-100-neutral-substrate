\section*{Appendix A. Glossary of Terms}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix A. Glossary of Terms}

The glossaries below are explanatory aids.
They do not introduce additional definitions, assumptions, or theorem conditions.
Where wording differs from the formal development,
Sections~\ref{sec:requirements} and~\ref{sec:formal-dev}
are authoritative.

Throughout these glossaries, the exclusion of causal and normative
commitments is stated at the level of substrate-layer assertion,
not vocabulary.
Causal and normative claims may be represented as
reified, attributed, provenance-bearing content;
they may not be asserted of substrate individuals
as substrate-layer facts.
