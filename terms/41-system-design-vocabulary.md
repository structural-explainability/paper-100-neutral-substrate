# System Design Vocabulary

Design Objective.
A specified goal or requirement that guides the structure of a system or representation.
Design objectives determine which properties a system must support and therefore which structural constraints apply.

Structural Constraint.
A restriction on the permissible structure of a system, imposed to ensure that the system satisfies a given design objective.
Structural constraints limit how entities, relations, or representations may be organized.

Design Context.
The problem setting and objectives within which a system or representation is constructed.
Design context determines which constraints and evaluation criteria are relevant.

Semantic Heterogeneity.
A condition in which different systems or communities use incompatible vocabularies, conceptualizations, or ontological commitments to describe the same domain.

Interoperability under Semantic Heterogeneity.
The ability of systems using different conceptual models or ontologies to exchange and use information without requiring full semantic agreement.

Structural Property.
A feature of a system that depends on the arrangement or relations of its elements, rather than on particular interpretations or domain meanings.

Representation System.
A structured framework used to encode entities, relations, or states of affairs for purposes such as reasoning, analysis, or interoperability.
