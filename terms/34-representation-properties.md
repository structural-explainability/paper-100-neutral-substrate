# Representation Properties

Extension.
An addition to a representation system that introduces further entities, relations, or interpretations.

Admissibility.
A condition under which an interpretation or extension satisfies the structural constraints of a representation system.

Invariance.
A property that remains unchanged under interpretation or extension.

Neutrality.
A property of a representation substrate in which the structure does not require commitment to specific ontological interpretations.

Interpretive Independence.
A property of a representation system in which representations do not require commitment to a particular interpretive framework.

Extension Stability.
A property of a representation system in which identity and structural conditions remain invariant under admissible extensions.
