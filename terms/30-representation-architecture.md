# Foundations of Representation Architecture in Ontology

Representation architecture describes how representations are organized
independently of their interpretation.

A representation architecture separates the structural layer that encodes
information from the interpretive frameworks that assign meaning to it.

Typical architectural layers include:

representation elements / representation system / representation substrate

Structural properties of the architecture determine how representations can
be extended, interpreted, and constrained.

This layer provides the infrastructure on which interpretive frameworks and
ontologies operate.
