# Logical / Formal Vocabulary

Primitive.
A symbol or term introduced without definition and treated as a basic element of a formal system.

Proposition.
A statement that can be either true or false within a logical system.
(Propositions are logical contents that may be asserted
by ontologies or derived by interpretive frameworks.)

Definition.
A statement that fixes the meaning of a term using previously established vocabulary.

Axiom.
A foundational statement assumed within a formal system from which other statements are derived.

Theorem.
A proposition logically derivable from axioms and definitions.

Interpretation.
An assignment of semantic meaning to the symbols of a formal system.

Model.
A structure in which the axioms of a theory are satisfied under an interpretation.
