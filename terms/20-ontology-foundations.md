# Ontology Foundations

Ontology foundations establish the basic conceptual assumptions used when
describing entities, relations, and categories.

These foundations clarify what kinds of things an ontology may represent and
how those things may be distinguished, related, and persisted.

Ontology foundations address:

- the nature of entities and categories
- principles of identity and persistence
- relations between entities
- distinctions between descriptive and normative domains
- meta-level assumptions about ontological commitment

These concepts provide the conceptual grounding for representation systems
that encode ontological structures.
